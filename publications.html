Current research (theory)

My current project is to develop PAC-Bayesian generalization theory for dependent time series under distribution shift, with computable risk bounds beyond the i.i.d. setting.

Current research (decision-making)

Another project is risk-controlled optimization under decision-dependent shift, using causal and counterfactual modeling to evaluate safety on post-decision distributions in sequential and portfolio decision problems.
